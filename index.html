About Lyons Contracting

Thomas Petrilli, owner. Alexandria roofer since 1996.

Lyons Contracting was founded by Tom Petrilli with a simple idea: Northern Virginia homeowners needed a more customer-centered roofing company. Too many outfits ignored the homeowner’s voice and installed whatever system they happened to have on the truck.

Tom still does the estimates. The same crew has been with him for years. Reviews mention next-day proposals, photo updates from the roof, a yard left clean, and no pressure to buy work you do not need.
