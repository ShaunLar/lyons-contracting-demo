About Lyons Contracting — your Alexandria, VA roofer

Founded in 1996, Lyons Contracting has become the roofer Alexandria and Falls Church homeowners keep calling — along with families in the surrounding Northern Virginia towns. When they turn to Lyons for a roof or a repair, they are not getting whatever system happened to be on the truck that week.

Lyons works with each client to choose the right roof for the house, then installs the full system to spec so the roof is built to last.

Owner

Thomas Petrilli, owner of Lyons Contracting

Lyons Contracting was founded by Tom Petrilli because the Northern Virginia residential roofing industry needed a more customer-centered approach. Too many companies were not taking the homeowner’s voice into account, and they installed the system they already had on hand.

Lyons offers traditional, contemporary, and custom roofing systems so clients have a real choice. Tom still does the estimates. The crew that shows up is the same crew that has been with the company for years.

That is the part reviews keep repeating: someone answers, someone shows up, the proposal is clear, and nobody pushes work you do not need.
